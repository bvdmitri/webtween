## [Unreleased]

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-23

### Added

- `slowDown` and `slowdownTimeline` take `segments`: several non-overlapping stretches of the
  source, each slowed by its own factor from 1 to 16, the source between them at its pace.
  `factor` with an optional `range` works as before.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-21

### Added

- `createInterpolator`: the frame at any time between two frames, generated by RIFE on WebGPU
  through onnxruntime-web, or on WebAssembly where there is no WebGPU.
- `slowDown`: a stream of decoded frames slowed by a factor from 1 to 16, over the whole video or
  a range, with progress and cancelling.
- `slowdownTimeline`: the slowdown's time map in seconds, both ways.
- `tools/export`: RIFE 4.25 exported from Practical-RIFE to ONNX, from hash-pinned inputs.
- The model ships in the package (`models/rife-4.25-fp32.onnx`, with its manifest), and
  `createInterpolator` uses it when no model is given; `MODEL_FILE`, `ONNXRUNTIME_WEB_VERSION` and
  `RUNTIME_FILES` name the files an application serves itself.
- A demo page that slows a picked clip down and downloads the MP4.
